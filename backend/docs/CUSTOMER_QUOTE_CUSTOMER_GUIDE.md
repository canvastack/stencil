## Receiving Your Quote

### What to Expect

After you submit an order request, our team will:
1. Review your requirements
2. Negotiate with suppliers
3. Prepare a detailed quotation
4. Send it to your email

### Email Notification

You'll receive an email with:
- **Subject**: "Your Quotation from [Company Name]"
- **Quote Summary**: Brief overview of items and total
- **PDF Attachment**: Detailed quotation document
- **Portal Link**: Secure link to view and respond
- **Expiry Date**: Deadline to respond (usually 7 days)

### Important Notes

⏰ **Time Sensitive**: Quotes are valid for a limited time (shown in email)  
🔒 **Secure Link**: Each quote has a unique secure link  
📧 **Check Spam**: If you don't receive it, check your spam/junk folder


## Viewing Your Quote

### Accessing the Quote

1. **Open the Email**: Find the quotation email in your inbox
2. **Click the Link**: Click "View Quote" button or copy the link
3. **Portal Opens**: Your quote opens in a secure browser window

### No Login Required (First Time)

- You can view and respond without creating an account
- Your unique link provides secure access
- Registration is optional but recommended

### What You'll See

The quote portal displays:
- **Quote Number**: Unique reference (e.g., CQ-202402-0001)
- **Quote Date**: When it was created
- **Valid Until**: Expiry date with countdown timer
- **Order Items**: Detailed list of products/services
- **Pricing**: Complete breakdown of costs
- **Terms**: Payment and delivery terms
- **Action Buttons**: Accept, Counter Offer, or Reject

## Understanding Your Quote

### Quote Header

```

Date: February 19, 2024

```

### Order Items Section

Each item shows:
- **Product Name**: What you're ordering
- **Specifications**: Size, material, customization details
- **Quantity**: Number of units
- **Unit Price**: Price per item
- **Total**: Quantity × Unit Price

Example:
```
Custom Etching Plate - Stainless Steel
Specifications: 10x15cm, Arial Bold font, "Company Logo"
Quantity: 2
Unit Price: Rp 750,000
Total: Rp 1,500,000
```

─────────────────────────────────

Tax (11%):          Rp   181,500
─────────────────────────────────

Typical payment structure:

## Accepting a Quote

### When to Accept

Accept the quote if:
- ✅ Price is within your budget
- ✅ Delivery timeline works for you
- ✅ Product specifications are correct
- ✅ Payment terms are acceptable
- ✅ You're ready to proceed

### Acceptance Process

#### Step 1: Review Everything

Before accepting:
- [ ] Verify all items and specifications
- [ ] Check total amount
- [ ] Understand payment terms
- [ ] Note delivery timeline
- [ ] Read terms and conditions

#### Step 2: Click Accept

1. Click the green **Accept Quote** button
2. Acceptance dialog appears

#### Step 3: Confirm Terms

The dialog shows:
- Total amount to pay
- Down payment amount (50%)
- Terms acceptance checkbox
- Cancel and Confirm buttons

#### Step 4: Accept Terms

1. Read the terms summary
2. Check the "I accept the terms and conditions" box
3. Click **Confirm Acceptance**

### What Happens Next

After you accept:

**Scenario A: Instant Approval (Most Common)**
- ✅ Quote approved immediately
- 📧 You receive payment instructions via email
- 💳 Payment link and bank details provided
- ⏰ Payment deadline specified
- 📄 Proforma invoice attached

**Scenario B: Pending Review (High-Value Orders)**
- ⏳ Quote sent for admin review
- 📧 You receive confirmation email
- ⏰ Review typically completed within 24 hours
- 📧 Payment instructions sent after approval

### Why Pending Review?

Some quotes require review for:
- High order value
- First-time customers
- Custom products
- Special requirements

This ensures we can fulfill your order perfectly!

## Submitting a Counter Offer

### When to Counter Offer

Consider a counter offer if:
- 💰 Price is slightly above your budget
- 🚚 You need different delivery terms
- 📦 You want to adjust quantities
- ⏰ You need faster/slower delivery

### Counter Offer Process

#### Step 1: Click Counter Offer

1. Click the yellow **Counter Offer** button
2. Counter offer form appears

#### Step 2: Enter Your Offer

**Counter Amount**
- Enter your proposed total amount
- Must be a positive number
- System shows difference from original

Example:
```
Original Quote:     Rp 1,831,500
Your Counter:       Rp 1,650,000
Difference:         -Rp 181,500 (-9.9%)
```

**Reason (Required)**
- Explain why you're countering
- Minimum 20 characters
- Be specific and polite

Good examples:
- "Budget constraint - can we reduce shipping cost?"
- "Willing to accept longer delivery for lower price"
- "Can we remove insurance to reduce total?"

Bad examples:
- "Too expensive" (too vague)
- "Lower price" (not specific)

**Additional Requests (Optional)**
- Any other adjustments you need
- Delivery preferences
- Payment term changes

Example:
- "Can we do 30% DP instead of 50%?"
- "Need delivery within 7 days if possible"

#### Step 3: Submit Counter

1. Review your counter offer
2. Click **Submit Counter Offer**
3. Confirmation message appears

### What Happens Next

After submitting:
- 📧 Admin receives your counter offer
- ⏰ Response typically within 24 hours
- 📧 You'll be notified of their response

### Possible Responses

**1. Counter Accepted**
- ✅ Your counter offer is approved
- 💳 Proceed to payment at new amount
- 📄 Updated invoice provided

**2. Counter Rejected**
- ❌ Counter offer declined
- 📝 Reason provided
- 💡 May suggest alternatives
- 🔄 Can submit new quote request

**3. New Counter Offer**
- 🔄 Admin proposes different amount
- 📝 Explanation provided
- ✅ You can accept, reject, or counter again

### Negotiation Rounds

- **Maximum Rounds**: Usually 3
- **After Max**: Can only accept or reject
- **Time Limit**: Quote expiry still applies

### Counter Offer Tips

✅ **DO:**
- Be reasonable and realistic
- Explain your reasoning clearly
- Suggest specific adjustments
- Be polite and professional
- Respond promptly

❌ **DON'T:**
- Lowball excessively
- Be vague or unclear
- Make unrealistic demands
- Take too long to respond
- Be rude or demanding


## Rejecting a Quote

### When to Reject

Reject the quote if:
- ❌ Price is too high for your budget
- ❌ Delivery timeline doesn't work
- ❌ Requirements have changed
- ❌ Found alternative supplier
- ❌ No longer need the product

### Rejection Process

#### Step 1: Click Reject

1. Click the red **Reject Quote** button
2. Rejection form appears

#### Step 2: Select Reason

Choose the main reason:
- 🔴 **Price too high**: Budget constraints
- ⏰ **Delivery too long**: Need it sooner
- 📝 **Changed requirements**: Specifications changed
- 🔍 **Found alternative**: Better option elsewhere
- ❓ **Other**: Specify your reason

#### Step 3: Add Comments (Optional)

Provide additional details:
- Specific concerns
- What would make you reconsider
- Feedback for improvement

Example:
"Price is above budget. Would reconsider if delivery time could be extended to reduce costs."

#### Step 4: Confirm Rejection

1. Review your reason
2. Click **Confirm Rejection**
3. Confirmation message appears

### What Happens Next

After rejection:
- 📧 Admin is notified with your reason
- 💡 They may contact you with alternatives
- 🔄 You may receive a revised quote
- ✅ No obligation to proceed

### Can I Change My Mind?

- Quote becomes read-only after rejection
- Contact admin if you want to reconsider
- They can create a new quote for you

## Making Payment

### After Quote Acceptance

Once your quote is approved, you'll receive:
- 📧 Payment instructions email
- 💳 Payment methods available
- 🏦 Bank account details
- 📄 Proforma invoice
- ⏰ Payment deadline

### Payment Methods

#### Bank Transfer (Primary Method)

**Step 1: Get Bank Details**
```

Account Number: 1234567890
Account Name: PT Example Company
Amount: Rp 915,750 (DP 50%)
Reference: CQ-202402-0001
```

**Step 2: Make Transfer**
- Use online banking or ATM
- Transfer exact amount
- Include quote number in notes

**Step 3: Upload Proof**
1. Take screenshot/photo of transfer receipt
2. Go to payment page
3. Click **Upload Payment Proof**
4. Select your file (JPG, PNG, or PDF)
5. Click **Upload**

**Step 4: Wait for Verification**
- Admin verifies payment (usually within 24 hours)
- You receive confirmation email
- Order proceeds to production

#### Credit Card / E-Wallet (If Available)

**Step 1: Click Payment Link**
- Open payment link from email
- Select payment method

**Step 2: Complete Payment**
- Enter payment details
- Confirm transaction
- Automatic verification

**Step 3: Confirmation**
- Instant confirmation
- Receipt emailed
- Order proceeds immediately

### Payment Timeline

```
Day 1: Quote Accepted
       ↓
       Payment Instructions Sent
       ↓
Day 1-3: Make Payment
       ↓
       Upload Proof (if bank transfer)
       ↓
Day 2-4: Payment Verified
       ↓
       Production Begins
```

### Payment Tips

✅ **DO:**
- Pay within deadline
- Upload clear payment proof
- Include quote number in transfer
- Keep receipt for records

❌ **DON'T:**
- Miss payment deadline

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How long is my quote valid?**  
A: Typically 7 days from the date sent. The exact expiry date is shown in your email and on the quote page.

**Q: Can I extend the quote validity?**  
A: Yes! Contact us before it expires, and we can extend it for you.

**Q: What if I miss the deadline?**  
A: The quote becomes read-only. Contact us to request a new quote with updated pricing.

**Q: Is the price negotiable?**  
A: Yes! You can submit a counter offer with your proposed price and reasoning.

**Q: How many times can I counter offer?**  
A: Usually up to 3 rounds of negotiation. After that, you can accept or reject the final offer.

### Payment Questions

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**  
A: Bank transfer (primary), and credit card/e-wallet if available. Details provided after quote acceptance.

**Q: Do I pay the full amount upfront?**  
A: Usually 50% down payment, then 50% balance before delivery. Terms are specified in your quote.

**Q: What if I can't pay within the deadline?**  
A: Contact us immediately. We may be able to extend the deadline or adjust payment terms.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**  
A: Yes! We use secure payment gateways and never store your full payment details.

**Q: Can I get a receipt?**  
A: Yes! You'll receive a digital receipt after payment verification, and a tax invoice after full payment.

### Order Questions

**Q: Can I modify my order after accepting?**  
A: Before payment, yes. After payment, changes may incur additional costs. Contact us to discuss.

**Q: How long does production take?**  
A: Delivery timeline is specified in your quote (e.g., "10-14 working days"). This starts after payment confirmation.

**Q: Can I track my order?**  
A: Yes! You'll receive tracking updates via email and can check status in the customer portal.

**Q: What if my order is delayed?**  
A: We'll notify you immediately and provide a new estimated delivery date.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**  
A: Before production starts, yes (may incur cancellation fee). After production, cancellation is not possible. See terms and conditions.

### Account Questions

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**  
A: No, you can view and respond to quotes without an account. However, registration provides additional benefits.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**  
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the reset instructions.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**  
A: Yes, update it in your profile settings. You'll need to verify the new email.

**Q: How do I unsubscribe from emails?**  
A: Click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any email, or manage preferences in your portal settings.

### Technical Questions

**Q: The quote link isn't working. What should I do?**  
A: The link may have expired. Contact us to resend the quote with a new link.

**Q: I can't upload my payment proof. Help!**  
A: Ensure your file is JPG, PNG, or PDF and under 5MB. Try a different browser or contact support.

**Q: The page isn't loading properly.**  
A: Try refreshing the page, clearing your browser cache, or using a different browser.

**Q: Is the portal mobile-friendly?**  
A: Yes! The portal works on all devices - desktop, tablet, and mobile.
